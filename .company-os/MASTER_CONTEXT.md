# Master context

Shalimar Fashions is a wholesale retailer platform. Shalimar Connect supports retailer relationships, inbox and messaging, campaigns, automations, and AI-assisted operations. Users include internal staff and wholesale retailers.

## Current stack
Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase, next-intl, Vitest, Recharts/Tremor and custom SVG charts. See `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and the source for current details.

## Integrations and safety
Supabase and WhatsApp-related provider integrations exist in the repository. Messaging must remain consent-first and approval-gated. No production credentials or live sends are used by this local OS.

Repository: `wacrm`; current branch is recorded by `company status`. The expected `docs/SHALIMAR_PRODUCT_BRIEF.md` is not present at installation time; product assumptions are therefore provisional and must be verified when the brief is added.

# Company rules

- Preserve original requests and existing work.
- Evidence is required before claims of completion, verification, security, deployment, or production readiness.
- Local, reversible, free, testable changes may proceed automatically.
- Real messages, production credentials/data, paid services, destructive migrations, DNS, and production deployment require explicit approval.
- Never commit secrets. Treat provider and external research as time-sensitive and record sources and dates.
- Separate specialist ownership in task/session records; do not claim native sessions when unavailable.

# Cost model

All figures below are planning estimates in INR, before GST, based on delivered Indian WhatsApp messages. They are not a purchase quote. Meta’s live rate card and the chosen provider’s markup must be rechecked on the day an account is enabled.

| Monthly delivered messages | Marketing at ₹1.09 | Utility/auth at ₹0.145 | Service after any free allowance |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5,000 | ₹5,450 | ₹725 | rate-card dependent |
| 25,000 | ₹27,250 | ₹3,625 | rate-card dependent |
| 100,000 | ₹109,000 | ₹14,500 | rate-card dependent |
| 250,000 | ₹272,500 | ₹36,250 | rate-card dependent |

Planning assumptions come from the AiSensy India page, which states ₹1.09 marketing and ₹0.145 utility/auth/service for Indian users effective 1 October 2026. Gupshup’s January 2026 PDF lists a different India utility/auth list rate of ₹0.115, demonstrating why the app stores provider and rate-card provenance instead of hard-coding a universal price.

Separate in every estimate: Meta/carrier fee, provider markup, subscription, seats, setup/KYC, number rental, AI usage, hosting/database/storage, GST and any DLT/SMS fees. The schema fields added in migration 043 keep estimated campaign cost and budget snapshot separate from delivery status.
